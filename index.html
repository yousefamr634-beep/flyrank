Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring & Decay Prediction

Author: Yousef Amr | Track: Search Intelligence & ML
Abstract: Digital publishing operations struggle to proactively identify organic search decay before severe traffic and impression drops occur. To address this, we evaluate a predictive decision-support pipeline built on the FlyRank ML Internship dataset to systematically score and rank content refresh opportunities. Using group-aware split validation (GroupKFold by content type), we evaluate a Random Forest Classifier against a historical heuristic baseline. Our model demonstrates operational decision-support utility with a measured validation ROC-AUC of 0.76, significantly outperforming the baseline while eliminating cross-vertical leakage. Finally, we translate these predictions into an actionable, prioritized editorial playbook with strict human-in-the-loop safeguards.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Maintaining organic search visibility requires active optimization, yet search content naturally decays due to algorithm evolution, competitor activity, and shifting user intent. To address this FlyRank content lane challenge, editorial teams require a quantitative framework to prioritize refresh workflows before traffic declines severely. This research establishes an automated scoring framework to transform search operations from reactive audits into proactive content refreshes.

2. Data Scope & Safety

The analysis utilizes the 79-million row FlyRank ML Internship dataset (Release: Q1-Q2). We primarily joined the search_performance_metrics and content_metadata tables, filtering for the 6-month date window capturing stable core updates. Public Safety & Exclusions: Client identities, private queries, domain names, and exact URLs were strictly excluded and hashed at the source to ensure absolute data privacy and public safety.

3. Methodology

Our methodology focuses on building a robust, leakage-free pipeline:

  • Assumptions & Label Definition: We assume a page requires a refresh if it exhibits a sustained 15%+ drop in impressions over 3 weeks. The target label (is_declining) is defined as a binary classification based on this trajectory.
  • Features: Key features include impression momentum, keyword competition metrics, CPC, and intent clusters.
  • Baseline: We established a non-ML heuristic baseline that flags content solely based on historical 30-day traffic averages.
  • Validation & Leakage Checks: To prevent cross-vertical data leakage, we discarded standard random splits and implemented a GroupKFold cross-validation strategy grouped strictly by content_type.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

This model serves strictly as a decision-support system and directional indicator. It does not provide causal guarantees that rewriting a flagged page will directly recover specific organic rankings. Furthermore, macro-level unobserved variables, such as unannounced major search engine core updates or seasonal spikes, lie outside our current feature boundaries.

6. Ranked Content Recommendations (Playbook)

Predictions are translated into a prioritized action playbook for editorial teams:

  • P1 - Urgent Refresh High traffic decay risk (Reason: HIGH_TRAFFIC_DECAY_RISK). Prioritize immediate structural rewrite.
  • P2 - Intent Realignment Moderate decay risk (Reason: MODERATE_DECAY_INTENT_DRIFT). Update FAQs and keyword targeting.
  • P3 - Preserve & Monitor Evergreen pages (Reason: STABLE_EVERGREEN). Do not alter core content.

Safeguard: High-converting transactional landing pages and legal policy pages are excluded from automated text generation and require full manual CRO review.
